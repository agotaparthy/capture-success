# NC IDEA Video — Face to Camera, ~90 Seconds

**The format they asked for:** you, talking to a friend. No b-roll, no production, no script-reading energy. ~250 words lands around 1:30–1:45 with natural pauses, safely inside the 1–2 minute window.

**Don't memorize the words — memorize the six beats.** Say each one however it comes out on that take. The version below is one way it sounds.

---

## The talk

> So last spring, a team of high schoolers from our first cohort won TiE Regionals — first place, and a spot at nationals in Seattle. And the wild part is — a year ago, those kids were building completely alone.
>
> That's the whole idea behind Capture Success. I'm a high schooler, my co-founders are high schoolers, and we got tired of watching really talented students sit on real ideas because they had nobody to build with. So we made the room ourselves.
>
> Right now that room is ten student companies. One of them, VisioCourt, is running live court tracking in three Town of Morrisville facilities — an actual town, actually using it — and they took second at DECA States and presented at MIT.
>
> And this fall we're running a free six-week accelerator at Frontier RTP. Six Mondays, any student ninth grade and up, ends with a live pitch night. We recruited twelve partners ourselves — UNC, Launch Chapel Hill, CED, Wake Tech, Hub RTP sponsors the venue, Jason's Deli feeds everybody.
>
> Here's why we need NC IDEA: the program is free, and it has to stay free, because the students who need it most are exactly the ones who can't pay. The grant covers the real stuff — dinner every session, materials so teams can prototype, pitch night, and running this again in the spring for more schools.
>
> Students will show up and build. We've proven that part. We just want North Carolina to be the best place in the country to be a student founder — and honestly, we're already building it.

---

## The six beats (memorize these, not the words)

1. **Hook:** Beacon won TYE Regionals and qualified for nationals. A year ago they were alone.
2. **The idea:** students run it, for students, because building alone is the real problem.
3. **Proof:** 10 companies · VisioCourt live in 3 Morrisville facilities, 2nd DECA States, presented at MIT.
4. **The program:** free 6-week accelerator at Frontier RTP, grades 9+, pitch night, 12 partners we recruited ourselves.
5. **The ask:** free has to stay free — grant funds dinner, materials, pitch night, spring cohort.
6. **Close:** students show up and build; make NC the best place to be a student founder.

## Delivery notes

- **First take is a warm-up, never the submission.** Do 4–5; pick the one where you sound like yourself, not the cleanest one.
- **First ten seconds decide everything** — start with the Beacon story, not "Hi, my name is." Your name is on the application already.
- If a take runs long, cut beat 3 down to just VisioCourt/Morrisville — it's the strongest proof.
- Look at the lens, not the screen. Phone at eye level, daylight in front of you, quiet room. Done.
- Every number here is on capturesuccess.org — nothing you'd have to walk back in diligence.
